DUEL TOOLS — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Duel Tools (the "Live Tracker" browser extension and this companion site) is a fan-made tool for tracking Yu-Gi-Oh! Goat Format duels on DuelingBook.com. This page explains what data it touches, where it goes, and what it's used for.

What stays on your device

Your DuelingBook username, deck/side lists, and tracker preferences are stored locally in your browser (via chrome.storage). This data never leaves your device unless Live Sync (below) is turned on, and is cleared if you remove the extension.

What Live Sync sends to our server

When you turn on Live Sync, the extension reads the duel page you're already looking at — the game log, and which zone each card is in (hand, field, graveyard, banished, etc.) — and sends that match state to our own server (duel-tools-kerem-production.up.railway.app) so it can power the live tracker view on this site. This is the same information already visible on your screen during the duel; nothing is read from anywhere else.

Live match data is used only to display your tracker and your opponent's tracker while a duel is in progress. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything beyond that feature.

What we never see or collect

  • Your DuelingBook password, account settings, or login credentials
  • Any activity outside DuelingBook.com — the extension does nothing on other sites
  • Payment information, real names, or other personal identifiers
  • Analytics, advertising trackers, or any data sold to third parties

Third-party lookups

The extension looks up card details (name, type, etc.) from the public YGOPRODeck card database by card name only. No personal or account data is attached to these requests.

Access

This extension is distributed privately to a specific group of approved players and is not available to the general public.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
