About the board view

This reconstructs the full duel field step by step from the replay's own action log, sized to fill the screen with no scrolling: Monster & Spell/Trap Zones, Graveyard and Banished piles (showing the actual last card sent there — click a pile to see everything in it), Deck/Extra counts, hand (toggle each player's visible below), and face-up/face-down + Attack/Defense position, all rendered with full card art. Field Spell, Pendulum, shared Extra Monster, and Skill Zones aren't shown — no real replay data has ever confirmed how (or whether) the action log names them, so rather than guess, those slots are left out entirely and that space goes to making everything else bigger.
